# IOS

## Outcome

A working iOS change verified on the relevant simulator or device surface.

## Method

Confirm project, scheme, destination, deployment target, and tool defaults; build and run; inspect UI hierarchy and logs; exercise states and interactions; use Instruments, traces, or memory graphs when triggered; handle App Intents and platform integration with current docs; report PASS, FAIL, or PARTIAL.

## Boundaries

Do not claim device behaviour from source inspection or let platform work override Apple design and accessibility decisions.
