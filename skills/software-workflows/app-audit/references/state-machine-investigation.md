# State-machine investigation

Use this method for flows where state changes or their ordering can materially affect correctness. Start with high-impact flows and scale to the task; a small stateless app may need no deeper model. Record selected flows, why they matter, and unexamined areas or unavailable evidence in `docs/review/APP-AUDIT.md` for an app audit. For focused debugging, use the existing task evidence instead; do not create an audit record solely for this method.

1. Find explicit states and implicit combinations of flags, records, derived values and pending work. Trace real entry points and owners across UI, persistence, background jobs and external boundaries. Use requirements and code to distinguish intended behaviour from observed behaviour; flag unresolved intent instead of inventing a rule.
2. For each selected flow, record meaningful states, invariants and transitions: event, starting state, guard, side effects, resulting state and relevant evidence. Use a compact table where helpful. Include failure and recovery paths, retries or redelivery, repeated actions, interruptions, and plausible concurrent ordering where relevant. Follow effects across boundaries and identify where state is authoritative.
3. Investigate whether a path can actually reach an invalid state or transition. Trace a concrete entry point and sequence, conditions, affected data and consequence; use safe isolated data and a repeatable test or controlled schedule when practical. A suspicious interleaving alone is a lead, not a confirmed failure. Separate source-supported reachability from reproduced behaviour and label unresolved assumptions or inaccessible evidence.
4. Prioritise confirmed defects by impact and group related fixes by shared cause. Fix clear defects within the invoking workflow's scope; retain a meaningful regression that exercises the failing sequence and intended outcome, including recovery or legitimate behaviour when relevant. Re-run it and update the model and evidence after the fix. Record larger or blocked work for follow-up; do not create tracker issues, publish, or deploy merely because this method was selected.

For App Audit, keep the model, evidence and findings together in the audit record, including post-fix results and residual risks; retain cheap deterministic passing checks in CI through its existing process. For focused debugging, keep the relevant evidence with the task and follow Debug’s existing verification scope. Do not claim exhaustive coverage or absence of bugs from a bounded investigation.
