# Compare

## Outcome

A defensible choice with the strongest elements and rejected tradeoffs visible.

## Method

1. Freeze the shared brief, evidence, constraints, intended fidelity, and comparison rubric before developing candidates.
2. Develop distinct candidates at that fidelity, directly or with the host's available agents. Keep each candidate independent until its output is complete.
3. Compare every completed candidate against the same rubric. Name hard-constraint failures and the trade-offs that decide the result.
4. Select the strongest viable base and incorporate only compatible strengths from other candidates, retaining the reasons for rejection.
5. Verify the synthesis against the brief and evidence. Return the selected artifact, comparison, decisive reasons, and remaining evidence gaps; label the result unresolved when a missing fact or preference prevents a defensible choice.

## Boundaries

Use ordinary research for simple option comparisons. This is for costly decisions where shallow pros and cons are insufficient.
