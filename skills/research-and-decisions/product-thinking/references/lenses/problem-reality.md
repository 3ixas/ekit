# Problem reality

Is there a real, recurring struggle, observed rather than imagined?

## Questions

- Who, by name or role, last hit this problem? When, and what did they do about it? (Fitzpatrick: ask about specifics in the past, not opinions about the future.)
- What are they using today, including a spreadsheet, a workaround, or doing nothing? (Moesta: the habit of the present is the solution to beat.)
- What was the struggling moment that made them look for something better? (Moesta: the push of the situation.)
- Did you notice this problem while living it, or think it up? (Graham: notice ideas instead of thinking them up; beware sitcom ideas that sound plausible but that nobody wants.)
- If this problem vanished tomorrow, what would change for them? (Christensen: the job the customer hires a product to do, including its social and emotional parts.)

## Failure signals

- Evidence is compliments, "I would use that", or friends' enthusiasm (Fitzpatrick: compliments and hypotheticals are bad data).
- No one has spent time, money, or effort working around the problem.
- The problem is described as a missing feature rather than a struggle.

## Passing looks like

Several specific people, a recent struggling moment, and a current workaround they dislike enough to have tried alternatives.
