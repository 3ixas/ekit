# Differentiation

Why this, over what people already use?

## Questions

- What would target customers use if this did not exist? List the real alternatives, including manual ones. (Dunford: positioning starts from competitive alternatives, not from the product.)
- What can this do that those alternatives cannot? Which of those differences do customers actually value, and can you show proof? (Dunford: unique attributes only matter through the value they create.)
- Who cares a lot about that value? (Dunford: best-fit customers are the ones who care most, not everyone who could use it.)
- What frame of reference would make the value obvious at a glance? (Dunford: the market category sets expectations before anyone reads further.)
- In a crowded market, is the first version substantially better for a narrow audience, not just different? (Artman, Linear: a hacky MVP fails where users already have good options.)

## Failure signals

- The differentiator is a feature an incumbent could ship in a sprint.
- The differentiator is real but nobody named in the earlier lenses cares about it.
- The pitch needs a paragraph before the value is clear.

## Passing looks like

One narrow differentiator, tied to value a specific segment already said they want, explainable in a sentence.
