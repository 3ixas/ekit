## Tensions worth surfacing

- **Seibel vs Artman** on MVP quality. Seibel's advice fits novel needs where users have no good option. Artman's fits crowded markets where they do. Ask which market the idea is in.
- **Graham and Chesky vs efficiency.** Unscalable, manual effort is a feature early on and a trap if it never gets automated. Ask what the manual work is meant to teach.
- **Saarinen vs Ellis and Vohra.** Linear leans on taste over metrics, while Superhuman measures product-market fit with a survey. Taste leads when the builder is the user; measurement leads when they are not.
