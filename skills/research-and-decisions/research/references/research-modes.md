# Research modes

## Current factual answer

Search the exact claim, prefer the responsible primary source, verify recency, and cite the page supporting each time-sensitive statement.

## Recommendation

Define the user's actual constraints and decision criteria before searching. Build a longlist, remove options that fail hard constraints, inspect the strongest remaining options in depth, and distinguish source facts from the final recommendation.

## Repository or skill discovery

Search across practitioners, official collections, and adjacent terminology. Pin each serious candidate, inventory every user-facing entrypoint, inspect licences and dependencies, then assess by capability rather than stars. Aggregators are discovery aids, not quality evidence.

## Recent practitioner evidence

Use only when current adoption, failures, or sentiment matter. Search multiple communities and first-party issue trackers; record the observation window; separate repeated reports from isolated anecdotes; verify technical claims against authoritative documentation.

## Deep research output

Keep a question ledger: answered, disputed, unknown, and irrelevant. Stop when additional sources repeat established evidence and remaining uncertainty no longer changes the decision. Preserve a local source-backed artifact when the work will be reused.
