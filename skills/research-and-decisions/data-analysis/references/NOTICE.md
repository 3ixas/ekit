# Source treatment

The maintained methods are independently authored. Public primary documents are reference-only sources; see [sources.json](sources.json) for reviewed identities and response digests. Their websites and contents retain their own terms. No upstream documentation text, example code, image or renderer is bundled. The root companion `licenses/primary-documentation-reference.txt` explains this boundary. Preserve it with a downloaded bundle.

The former restricted OpenAI snapshot is excluded from this bundle, not relicensed or paraphrased into a replacement. Its original contents remain in the separate private preservation record. No permission to redistribute that snapshot is claimed. Eligible original contributions follow the repository terms; other retained sources retain their own notices.
