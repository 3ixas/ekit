# Ideate

## Outcome

Elias holding three strong, genuinely different ideas side by side, with the reasoning behind them and the rejected ones visible on request, ready to pick one to develop.

## Method

1. **Identify the subject.** If what to ideate about is unclear, ask, offering "surprise me" as a real option. If it takes more than three questions to pin down, ideation is the wrong workflow; say so.
2. **Ground before generating.** For software, read the relevant repository: product docs, `STRATEGY.md`, `GLOSSARY.md`, recent changes, and open issues. For anything else, gather the facts that constrain good ideas, using `research` when current external facts matter. If grounding fails, say what is missing and proceed.
3. **Restate the job, then split into axes.** First restate the subject as the activity it must accomplish, not the artefact ("a quarterly review" becomes "persuading busy people about cause and effect and deciding under uncertainty in 30 minutes"), and name the outcome one level above it. Then name 3–5 orthogonal aspects to think along, such as audience, job, mechanism, business model, distribution, or format, with at least one borrowed from an adjacent discipline's methods. Skip the axes for an atomic subject and say why.
4. **Generate widely.** Produce the full candidate list before judging any of it, covering every axis, with at least one idea per axis that is uncomfortable or unexpected and at least one that serves the outcome one level up. Aim for 15–30 candidates.
5. **Critique everything.** Judge each candidate on grounding in the evidence, distinctiveness, value to the intended person, and feasibility. Reject explicitly, with a one-line reason each. This is a cull, not an optimistic ranking.
6. **Present an options round.** Show the top three survivors as `grilling`'s options round: side by side, each diverging on a named axis, one of them a stretch option, with a recommendation. Offer the full ranked list and the rejections on request. Elias can pick, combine, reject all, or ask for another round along a different axis.
7. **Hand off the chosen idea.** To validate whether it is worth building, use `product-thinking`. When it is already settled and needs a software scope, use `grill-with-docs`. Never skip from ideas straight to planning or code.

When Elias approves, save the chosen idea, the three options, and the exploration trail to the idea's home: the project's docs in a repository, otherwise a vault note through `obsidian`.

## Boundaries

No requirements, plans, or code. Questions about solution direction, constraints, audience, or success criteria belong to the skill that receives the hand-off.
