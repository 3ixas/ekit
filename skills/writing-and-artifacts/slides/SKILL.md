# Slides

Build an editable presentation that helps its audience do something specific, then inspect every rendered slide. Read [presentation workflow](references/workflow.md) for story, construction and rendering checks.

1. Establish audience, decision or activity, delivery setting, length and requested format. Write a short narrative and assign each slide a distinct contribution; use `writing` for copy and `data-visualization` when a chart needs specialist judgement.
2. Inspect an existing deck or reference system before changing it. Discover an available presentation tool and rendering route. Preserve the original and the requested editable format.
3. Compose slides deliberately: readable hierarchy, meaningful evidence, consistent alignment and spacing, and a visual treatment suited to each slide's job. Preserve sources, units and uncertainty behind claims.
4. Save and reopen the deck. Render every slide, inspect for overflow and audience-distance readability, then revise observed problems and render the changed slides again.
5. Deliver the deck path and rendering evidence with any font, engine, animation or media limitations. A valid PPTX and a filled template are intermediate outputs, not proof of a finished presentation.

Local construction does not grant authority to publish, upload assets, use unlicensed imagery or share a deck. If no renderer is available, label the result as unverified rather than claiming that its slides were inspected.
