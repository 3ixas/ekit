# Ui Polish

## Outcome

A visibly stronger interface that preserves the product's established direction and behaviour.

## Method

Inspect rendered baseline and references before editing where feasible; choose relevant audit, polish, redesign, typography, colour, layout, surface, copy, native-feel, mobile-web, or hardening mode; fix the highest-impact craft problems in coherent batches; test content extremes and responsive states. For material visual changes, compare before and after at matching routes, states, viewports and representative data; report an unavailable baseline rather than implying a comparison. Screenshots supplement interaction and accessibility proof.

Read [polish modes](references/polish-modes.md) to choose the useful lenses and stopping criteria. Apply only modes supported by observed problems.

Use interface-design's shared Taste toolkit for source lint, browser measurements and screenshot critique when relevant; load that skill's `references/taste-toolkit.md` for commands and project policy. Preserve the approved direction and inspect website versus app composition by its own criteria. A polish pass does not redraw or reopen approved art direction.

When a render suggests clipping, collisions, scrolling, contrast or asset failures, use [rendered diagnostics](references/rendered-diagnostics.md) to collect targeted browser evidence without turning aesthetic preferences into blockers.

## Boundaries

Escalate wrong flow, information architecture, or visual foundation to interface-design; route material animation to motion.
