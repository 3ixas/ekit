# Apple Design

## Outcome

An Apple interface that feels native to its target platform and product rather than merely styled like Apple.

## Method

Identify platform, device, input, navigation, information density, states, and accessibility; consult current HIG; use references and existing app language; distinguish shared Apple principles from platform-specific behaviour; review adaptation, controls, typography, materials, motion, and system integration.

Read [Apple platform design](references/apple-platform-design.md) for platform distinctions, system integration, and a native-feel review.

For Apple-quality feel on the web (instant response, 1:1 direct manipulation, interruptible springs, velocity handoff, momentum, rubber-banding, materials, and optical typography), read [Emil Kowalski's translation for the web](references/source-methods/emil-apple-design.md).

## Boundaries

This owns design judgment, not Swift implementation, Xcode operation, or App Store submission.
