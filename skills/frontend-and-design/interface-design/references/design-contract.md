# Design contract

`DESIGN.md` is the project's durable record of its direction and implementation handoff. Keep it proportionate to the decision; link existing evidence rather than duplicate it.

## Minimum for substantial work

Record the product and audience, primary job and journey, scope and target surfaces, constraints and success criteria. List selected references with their role, source, useful qualities and what must not be copied. Record the chosen direction, product-specific signature or deliberate restraint, anti-goals, relevant tokens and interaction rules, one functional icon family, interface copy rules for clear actions, errors, empty and loading states, required screens and states, responsive and accessibility needs, motion and reduced-motion requirements, and acceptance criteria. Include open decisions, the evidence reviewed, the user's approval and links to approved screens or screenshots. Mark values estimated from screenshots as estimates; do not invent missing content or measurements.

Keep an exploration trail for each options round: options and axes, selection and why alternatives lost. Record the exploration surface and links, or why exploration was skipped and what evidence supports the decision. A provisional written direction can guide exploration; user sign-off on new or materially changed consequential decisions is required before implementation; retain recorded approval when the direction is unchanged. If rendering is unavailable, record the gap rather than claiming visual verification.

## Add when needed

- **Redesign:** Follow [redesign preservation](redesign-preservation.md); link the baseline, authorized changes, behaviour checks and before/after evidence.
- **Distinctive material or interaction:** Map the important regions to their implementation medium, real asset source, responsive and accessibility treatment, and any material change from the approved concept. Explain missing assets and obtain agreement on a changed direction; do not quietly substitute one.
- **Taste toolkit:** Record only decisions actually used: website subject and claims or app question, finding and action; data assumptions; chosen assets and direction; and intentional exceptions. Link any supporting sheet or policy. Approved project decisions govern generated records.
- **Service exploration:** Record the canvas or project, design system, screens, relevant feedback and open decisions. Existing projects with a `Stitch exploration record` heading may retain it.

The handoff must give implementers the approved hierarchy, tokens, states, copy and assets, responsive behaviour, accessibility, motion, acceptance evidence and links where relevant. A small settled edit can reuse the existing contract without creating a new one.
