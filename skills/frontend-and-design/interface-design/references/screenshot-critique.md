# Screenshot critique

Inspect rendered screens, not source or intent. Use representative target sizes and relevant states; for responsive web work include desktop, tablet and phone where applicable. Record what was captured and any unavailable surfaces. When supported, a fresh-context reviewer can use the screenshots, brief, selected references and `DESIGN.md` without the builder's rationale.

## Shared checks

Check the user's main task and next action, hierarchy, readable content, coherence with the approved direction, responsive changes, and collisions or clipping. Inspect relevant empty, loading, error and long-content states. Check keyboard, accessibility and actual interactions on the real surface; screenshots alone cannot prove them.

## Choose a lens

- **Website:** Is the subject and main message clear? Does the composition, imagery, copy and signature support the claims throughout the page? Does the phone layout work as a composition? Compare sections, spacing, alignment and type with the references.
- **App:** Can someone find the important information and take the next action? Is density suitable for the work, are labels and data legible and consistent, and do the relevant states and controls make sense? Check representative tasks and content at each supported size.

Counts of type sizes, alignment edges or repeated layouts are prompts for investigation, not acceptance thresholds. Judge them against the product, content, accessibility and approved direction; do not force a marketing-page layout onto an app.

## Fix and stop

Name the largest material problems, fix them from the project decisions, recapture and repeat for at most three rounds. Report unresolved findings and unverified states. Do not remove familiar patterns without replacing any lost content or function.
